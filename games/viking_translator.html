Who were the Vikings?

The Vikings were the people that were trading and raiding from about the end of the 8th century to the mid-11th century. The Vikings had a writing system which was formed of runes, which were used to write names on property, or on commemorative rune stones. Some of the characters that this tool uses are the same as the Viking runes, although others just look like they could have been. Keep in mind that this tool does not actually translate text, but simply encodes each latin character to a Viking-like character. The result is more of an aesthetic encoding rather than a reproduction of how Vikings wrote, or a safe way to encrypt a message.
