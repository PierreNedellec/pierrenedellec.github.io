On being bad at things on purpose

I keep a list of things I am deliberately bad at. Not out of modesty — out of budget. There is only so much attention to go round, and being fluent in six things means being unbearable in all of them.

The interesting part is what happens at the edges. The photography made the writing tighter. The writing made the code legible. None of these were planned; they were the residue of doing unrelated things badly for long enough.

A generalist is just someone who has not yet been asked to specialise by someone they respect.

So this site is the list. Six directories, no hierarchy between them, and a CV for the people who need one.
