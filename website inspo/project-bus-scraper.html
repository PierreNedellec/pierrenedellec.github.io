A bus-timetable scraper nobody asked for

My bus is unreliable in a very specific way. I wanted the shape of that unreliability, not the schedule.

hero — screenshot of the delay chart
rolebuilt all of it, alone
spansix weekends
outcome112 days of data

01 — the problem

The operator publishes a timetable and a live feed, but never the difference between them. I scraped both every two minutes for four months and stored the gap.

02 — what broke

Everything, twice. The markup changed in March and I lost nine days before I noticed. Now the job emails me when a scrape returns a suspiciously round number of rows.

detail — the schema, or the failure email

03 — what i'd do differently

Write the alerting first. A dataset with a hole in it is a worse artefact than no dataset, and I only found that out the expensive way.
