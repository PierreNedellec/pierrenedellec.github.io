How to use this tool

Enter the start and end time of your lesson, and press the calculate button! Then the progress bar will turn blue and show you how far through the lesson you are.

Why should I use this?

Sometimes school is boring. If you're too bored to listen to your teacher, you can stare at the ticking of the progress bar ticking toward the end of the lesson.
