about

I'm a student, which is a polite way of saying I have time and no money. I read economics because it is the only subject that lets you be nosy about everything and call it methodology.

Outside of that: I edit the comment section of the student paper, I photograph multi-storey car parks with more sincerity than the subject deserves, and I write small programs to answer questions nobody else has.

This site exists because I got tired of sending four links. If you're here from my CV, the projects are the honest part.
